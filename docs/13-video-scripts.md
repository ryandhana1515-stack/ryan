# 13. Video Scripts — Demo Video + Reseller Vision Video

Two ready-to-record scripts. Record your screen with **Loom** (free,
loom.com — records screen + your face bubble + voice in one click) or OBS
Studio. Speak the VOICE column while doing the SCREEN column. Keep energy
up; re-record a scene if you stumble — short scenes make editing easy.
If you prefer, hire a Fiverr editor and hand them this script plus your
raw screen recordings.

---

## 13.3 Recording tips (novice-friendly)

1. **Loom** is the fastest: sign up free → "New recording" → Screen +
   Camera → record scene by scene. It hosts the video and gives you a link
   too.
2. Phone mic in a quiet room beats a bad webcam mic. Speak 10% slower than
   feels natural.
3. Zoom your browser to 125% so the dashboard is readable on phones.
4. Use demo data that looks alive (run `python -m app.demo` style flows or
   send a few test leads first so numbers aren't zero).
5. Cut versions: full (YouTube), 60s (Reels/TikTok — scenes 4-6 of Video A),
   30s teaser (scene 5 + CTA).
6. Same script, other languages: record the English one, then use the
   Content AI to translate the script and re-record — or use an AI dubbing
   tool later. Start with English + one local language.
