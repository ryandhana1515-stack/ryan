# 16. Meet Your 15 AI Employees — The Pitch Sheet

One paragraph per employee: what it does, and what that means for the
business owner's money and time. Use this in demos, proposals, and the
website.

## The front office (they make you money)

**1. Sales AI — your closer who never sleeps.**
Replies to every lead in seconds — WhatsApp, web chat, 2am included. Asks
smart questions, spots buying signals, handles "too expensive," scores
every lead 0–100, and pushes for the booking. Hands hot or tricky
customers to a human with the full story attached.
*Means: no lead ever lost to a slow reply — the #1 leak in every SME.*

**2. Support AI — your customer service department.**
Answers customer questions 24/7 using ONLY your own prices, policies, and
FAQs — it can't invent things. Opens tickets for real problems, escalates
angry customers to you instantly, follows up next day.
*Means: happy customers at midnight without a night-shift salary.*

**3. Voice AI — your phone team.**
Writes and runs call scripts: appointment confirmations, missed-call
callbacks, follow-ups, re-activating old customers. Polite, human-like,
tireless.
*Means: no-shows drop, and your old customer list becomes money again.*

## The marketing department (they bring the leads)

**4. Marketing AI — your marketing director.**
Builds monthly marketing plans, campaign ideas, content calendars,
customer personas, competitor and trend research — grounded in your real
performance numbers, not guesses.
*Means: a real strategy every month, not random posting.*

**5. Branding AI — your brand strategist.**
Interviews you, then writes your mission, story, brand voice, positioning,
and naming options. Every other AI employee then obeys that brand voice.
*Means: everything you publish finally sounds like one company.*

**6. Content AI — your copywriter.**
Writes Instagram/Facebook posts, TikTok scripts with hooks, blogs, emails,
WhatsApp broadcasts, ad copy — platform-native, in your voice, in any
language.
*Means: a month of content from one instruction.*

**7. Social Media AI — your community manager.**
Drafts replies to comments and DMs, keeps heated threads out of public,
spots buying interest in comments and flags it as a lead, and writes your
weekly engagement report.
*Means: your comment section becomes a sales channel.*

**8. Image AI — your designer.**
Turns a plain request ("July promo visual, elegant, our colors") into a
professional image generation prompt and produces the visuals.
*Means: promo graphics in minutes, no designer invoice.*

**9. Video AI — your video producer.**
Same, for short video clips: product teasers, promo reels.
*Means: scroll-stopping motion content without an agency.*

**10. Website AI — your web developer.**
Generates complete, ready-to-publish landing pages in your brand voice —
hero, benefits, offer, FAQ, call-to-action.
*Means: a campaign page for tomorrow's promo, made tonight.*

## The back office (they protect you)

**11. Finance AI — your finance analyst.**
Turns your numbers into plain-English reports: what drove revenue, where
costs moved, cash runway, who owes you money, and three ranked
recommendations. Never moves money on its own — by design.
*Means: CFO-level clarity without CFO-level salary.*

**12. HR AI — your HR assistant.**
Scores resumes against the job fairly, drafts interview questions,
onboarding checklists, and performance reviews; answers staff policy
questions.
*Means: hiring stops eating your weekends.*

**13. SOP AI — your operations manual writer.**
Interviews you about any process and writes the step-by-step procedure a
new hire can follow on day one; answers staff "how do I…?" questions from
your documents, with citations.
*Means: your business stops living only inside your head — which is also
what makes it sellable.*

**14. Analytics AI — your business analyst.**
Reads the live numbers every day and tells you the ONE thing that matters,
anomalies with causes, and your Business Health Score. No filler, no
flattery.
*Means: you run the business from one honest morning briefing.*

## The boss of them all

**15. Manager AI — your chief of staff.**
The one you talk to, like texting a real manager. It researches your live
business data before answering, delegates work to the right employee,
reviews the team's performance with real numbers ("who's earning
autonomy, who's erroring"), and reports back in plain language.
*Means: you manage 14 AI employees by having one conversation.*

---

**The line that sells it:** "You're not buying software. You're hiring a
$4,100/month team for $49 — and the whole team reports to one manager,
who reports to you."
