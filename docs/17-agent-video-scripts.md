# 17. Agent Video Scripts — 15 Shorts + 1 Master YouTube Video

Workflow: record → upload to YouTube → send me the link (or paste the
video ID into `AGENT_VIDEOS` in `app/static/landing.html`) → the video
appears when visitors click that agent's card. Same clips post natively to
TikTok/IG/FB Reels.

Format for the 15 shorts: 25–40 seconds each, vertical, selfie or screen
recording, captions on. Formula: HOOK (3s) → SHOW IT (15-25s) → PUNCHLINE
+ CTA (5s). CTA is always: "Free 14 days — link in bio."

## The 15 shorts

**1. Sales AI** — Hook: "My best salesperson has never slept. Ever."
Show: test lead comes in → instant reply → appointment booked on dashboard.
Punch: "8 seconds. 2am. Booked. Your leads are dying in your inbox right now."

**2. Support AI** — Hook: "A customer complained at midnight. It was handled by 12:01."
Show: send "I can't log in / is this treatment painful?" → correct answer from knowledge base.
Punch: "It only answers from MY policies — it can't make things up."

**3. Voice AI** — Hook: "This phone call was made by nobody."
Show: play the confirmation call script / show call summary on screen.
Punch: "Every appointment confirmed. My no-shows dropped off a cliff."

**4. Marketing AI** — Hook: "I fired my marketing agency. My new one costs $1.60 a day."
Show: "Plan my July campaign" → full plan appears.
Punch: "Strategy from my REAL numbers, not vibes."

**5. Branding AI** — Hook: "My business had no story. AI interviewed me and wrote one."
Show: brand kit output (mission, voice, story).
Punch: "Now every post, every reply, sounds like ME."

**6. Content AI** — Hook: "30 days of content in 47 seconds. Watch."
Show: "Write a week of posts about brow lamination" → results scroll.
Punch: "In my voice. In any language. Never runs out of ideas."

**7. Social Media AI** — Hook: "There's money hiding in your comment section."
Show: comment "how much?" → AI drafts reply + flags it as a lead.
Punch: "It found 3 customers in my DMs I would've scrolled past."

**8. Image AI** — Hook: "I typed one sentence. I got this ad."
Show: prompt → generated visual appears.
Punch: "No designer. No Canva marathon. One sentence."

**9. Video AI** — Hook: "This clip? Made by my AI while I made coffee."
Show: generated promo clip playing.
Punch: "Reels on demand. My feed never goes quiet."

**10. Website AI** — Hook: "I needed a promo page by tomorrow. It took 3 minutes."
Show: "Make a landing page for my July promo" → open the generated page.
Punch: "Complete page. Brand voice. Overnight — without the overnight."

**11. Finance AI** — Hook: "I finally understand my own money."
Show: monthly report in plain English (revenue drivers, who owes).
Punch: "No spreadsheets. No accountant-speak. Just: here's what's happening."

**12. HR AI** — Hook: "47 resumes. Screened before my coffee got cold."
Show: screening scores + interview questions generated.
Punch: "Fair, fast, and it never gets tired on resume #40."

**13. SOP AI** — Hook: "My business used to live in my head. That was the problem."
Show: "Write the opening procedure" → step-by-step SOP appears.
Punch: "Now anyone can run my shop. THAT's what makes a business sellable."

**14. Analytics AI** — Hook: "My business texts me every morning. Honestly."
Show: daily digest ("Zero revenue activity — pipeline dry" style honesty).
Punch: "It doesn't flatter me. It tells me the ONE thing to fix today."

**15. Manager AI** — Hook: "I manage 14 employees with one text message."
Show: chat: "How's my team doing?" → real stats reply; "make me an image" → delegates.
Punch: "One conversation. A whole company moves. That's OmniX."

## Daily posting rhythm ("UGC videos every day")

Post ONE agent short per day for 15 days — that's half a month of daily
content from one recording session (batch-record 5 per sitting, ~90 min).
Repeat the cycle with new hooks; the scripts above each have room for 2–3
hook variations. Best posting order: Sales → Content → Image → Analytics →
Manager first (the most visual "wow" ones), then the rest.
