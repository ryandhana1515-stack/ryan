Everything points to one link: **your-app-url/offer** (the Founding Member
page). Personalize the [brackets]. Honesty rule: the "10 seats / price
rises to $149" claims on the offer page are REAL commitments — update the
seat counter as seats fill, and actually raise the price after.

## 15.4 The UGC video script — "POV: my business runs itself" (45–60s)

UGC = "user generated content" style: filmed like a normal person talking
to their phone camera, NOT an ad. These outperform polished ads on
TikTok/IG/Facebook because they feel like a friend's recommendation.

**Format:** vertical (9:16), selfie camera, natural light, real locations
in your salon. Jump-cut between shots. Captions on (most watch muted).

| Shot | Visual | You say (natural, not scripted-sounding) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 HOOK (0-3s) | Selfie cam, walking into your salon | "My business answered 23 customers last night. I was asleep." |
| 2 (3-10s) | Flip cam: show phone, WhatsApp full of replied chats | "This used to be me — replying DMs at 1am, losing customers when I was slow. Look at it now. Every single one answered in seconds." |
| 3 (10-20s) | Screen recording: dashboard, lead appears, AI reply | "I gave my business an AI team. Not a chatbot — a whole team. This one does sales. It just booked this lady for Thursday 3pm. I did nothing." |
| 4 (20-30s) | Screen recording: Manager AI making content/image | "This one writes my posts and makes my promo pictures. I literally text it like a manager: 'make me a July promo' — done." |
| 5 (30-40s) | Selfie cam, sitting, coffee | "The math is stupid: a receptionist is $1,800 a month and goes home at 6. This is $49 and never sleeps. In any language." |
| 6 CTA (40-50s) | Selfie cam, point down | "I'm opening it to 10 business owners before public launch — founding price locked forever. Link in bio. If it doesn't book you real appointments in 14 days, it's free." |

**3 alternative hooks to test (change only Shot 1):**
- "I fired myself from my own front desk."
- "POV: a customer messages your salon at 2:47am… and gets booked."
- "This AI made me $[X] while I was sleeping. Here's proof."

**Don't want to be on camera?** Two options: (1) film only hands/screens
and voice-over the same script; (2) AI avatar tools (e.g. HeyGen,
Captions) can turn this script into a talking-head video — decent for
testing, but your real face converts better in a trust business.

**Cut-downs:** 60s full → 30s (shots 1,3,5,6) → 15s teaser (shots 1,6).
Post natively on TikTok, IG Reels, FB Reels; pin to profile; run the
winner as a $10-20/day ad later.
