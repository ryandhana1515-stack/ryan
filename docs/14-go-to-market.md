# 14. Go-To-Market Playbook — Selling OmniX AI (first-time software seller)

You already know how to run a business. Selling software is the same game
with one twist: **you sell the outcome, and the product renews itself every
month.** This is the field playbook.

## 14.1 The only strategy that works at the start: Proof → Neighbors → Scale

### Phase A — Proof (Weeks 1–4): Brow Revolution IS the pitch
1. Run OmniX on Brow Revolution for real: leads, bookings, reminders, content.
2. Collect 4 numbers weekly (screenshot the dashboard):
   - average reply time to a new lead (before: hours → after: seconds)
   - appointments booked by AI
   - no-shows saved by reminders
   - hours of admin/content work saved
3. That dashboard screenshot + your story = your entire sales deck.
   "I automated my own beauty business" beats any brochure.

### Phase B — First 10 customers (Weeks 3–8): founder-led, zero ads
Your buyers are people like you: SG/MY beauty bosses (brows, lashes, nails,
facial, hair, aesthetics). You already know 50 of them.

WhatsApp pitch that works (send personally, one by one):

> "Hey [name], quick one — I built an AI system for Brow Revolution that
> now replies to all my IG/WhatsApp leads in seconds and books the
> appointments itself, even at 2am. Saved me [X] hours last week and
> caught [Y] bookings I would've missed. I'm opening it to 10 owners I
> know before public launch. Want a 15-min look at my live dashboard?"

Rules:
- **Demo on YOUR live system**, not slides. Send a test message, let them
  watch the AI reply and the lead appear on the dashboard.
- **Founding-customer offer:** first 10 pay $49/mo locked for life on the
  Growth plan (normally $149) in exchange for a testimonial + case study.
  Scarcity is real: exactly 10.
- **White-glove onboarding:** you set up their knowledge base with them on
  a call (30–60 min). At this stage YOU are the onboarding wizard.
- Ask every happy customer: "Which 2 owner friends should I show this to?"

### Phase C — Content engine (Weeks 6+): sell in public
- Record the demo video (script: doc 13) — pin it on TikTok/IG/FB.
- Post weekly "build in public" clips: real dashboard numbers, before/after
  stories ("AI booked 11 appointments while I slept").
- Bio link everywhere → /welcome → /signup (free trial).
- Run small Meta ads to beauty-business owners ($10–20/day) once organic
  posts show which message converts.

### Phase D — Resellers (Month 3+): other people sell for you
- Best reseller recruits: freelance social-media managers and small
  marketing agencies already serving beauty/service SMEs — they have the
  clients, you give them the product (doc 11 §11.3 pricing).
- Reseller pitch: "Stop selling posts. Sell an AI employee for $199/mo —
  it costs you $25. Your clients stay forever because it runs their business."
- Give resellers: demo video, pitch deck, this playbook, a sandbox account.

## 14.2 Objection answers (memorize these five)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already use Respond.io / ManyChat" | "Those are chat inboxes — a tool. This is a team: it sells, books, reminds, writes your content, makes your images, calls customers, and reports your numbers. And it costs less." |
| "What if the AI says something wrong?" | "It starts in Approve mode — it drafts, you tap approve. Nothing goes out without you until YOU decide to trust it. And it only answers from YOUR price list and policies." |
| "My customers want to talk to a human" | "They want an answer in 10 seconds. The AI hands hot or upset customers to you instantly, with the full conversation summary. You do the human part — closing." |
| "Too expensive" | "It's $5/day. One saved no-show pays the week. One extra booking pays the month. What's a missed 2am lead worth?" |
| "Is my data safe?" | "Your data sits in your own locked account, PDPA-compliant, never shared or sold. You can export and leave anytime." |

## 14.3 The weekly rhythm (2–3 hours/week while running Brow Revolution)

- Monday: screenshot dashboard numbers → 1 social post
- Tuesday: 5 personal WhatsApp pitches from your contact list
- Wednesday: 1 demo call (15 min each)
- Friday: onboard whoever said yes; ask for 2 referrals
- Target: 2 new customers/week → 20+ customers by month 3 → ~$2–3k MRR →
  then recruit your first 3 resellers.

## 14.4 What NOT to do (first-timer traps)

1. Don't build more features before 10 paying customers — sell what exists.
2. Don't discount below $49 — cheap customers churn hardest and demand most.
3. Don't chase enterprise/big brands yet — 6-month sales cycles will kill
   your momentum. SME owners decide in one WhatsApp conversation.
4. Don't run paid ads before one organic message has proven it converts.
5. Don't skip the testimonial ask — social proof is the compounding asset.
