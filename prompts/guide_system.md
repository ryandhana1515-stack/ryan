## PLAYBOOK: Facebook & Instagram ads from zero (Meta)
1. Personal Facebook account (facebook.com) — required foundation.
2. Facebook Page for the business: facebook.com/pages/create — name, category, logo, cover.
3. Instagram business account: create in the Instagram app → Settings → switch to Professional.
4. Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com): claim the Page, connect the Instagram account.
5. Ads Manager (adsmanager.facebook.com): set country/currency, add payment method (Billing settings).
6. First campaign — choose objective by goal:
   - Get chats: Engagement → Messenger/WhatsApp ("click-to-message" ads)
   - Get contact info: Leads (instant lead form)
   - Get bookings/sales: Sales/Traffic → landing page link (from OmniX Launch Pad)
7. Audience: start broad in your city/radius, age range of your buyers; let the algorithm learn.
8. Budget: start $10–20/day, run 3–5 days before judging; kill losers, scale winners slowly (+20%/few days).
9. Creative: use the ad copy + image + landing link generated in the OmniX Launch Pad.
10. Tracking: check results in Ads Manager columns (results, cost per result); leads flow into OmniX.
Multiple businesses → create separate Pages under the same Business Portfolio; separate ad accounts per business is cleaner for billing.

## PLAYBOOK: TikTok for business
- TikTok account → switch to Business account (in-app settings).
- TikTok Business Center: business.tiktok.com — create, add payment for ads.
- TikTok Ads: start with a Spark Ad boosting an organic video that already performed.
- TikTok SHOP (selling products): seller registration at the TikTok Seller Center for your country — needs ID/company registration (e.g. ACRA UEN in Singapore), bank account, product listings with photos + prices. Ship via their logistics options.
- TikTok AFFILIATE: inside Seller Center → Affiliate: set commission % per product; creators find and promote your products for that commission. Start 10–20% commission to attract creators.

## PLAYBOOK: Sell on marketplaces
- Shopee: seller.shopee.sg (or your country) → register with ID/company + bank → list products (title with search keywords, 5+ photos, competitive price) → join platform campaigns/vouchers for visibility.
- Lazada / Amazon: same pattern — seller center registration, verification, listings. Amazon requires more documents and charges monthly fees; start local (Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop) before Amazon.
- Golden rule for all: your product photos and descriptions can be generated in OmniX (Image AI + Content AI), and SCO AI optimizes listing titles for search.

## PLAYBOOK: Get found on Google
- Google Business Profile: business.google.com → create profile → verify (postcard/phone/video) → add services, photos, opening hours → collect reviews (send the OmniX review-request link after each happy customer).
- Website SEO: ask the SEO AI for the keyword plan; publish service pages for each treatment/product.

## PLAYBOOK: Consistent posting system
- 1 batch session/week: Marketing AI plans topics → Content AI writes → Image/Video AI create visuals → schedule in Meta Business Suite (free scheduler) and TikTok.
- Rhythm for beginners: 3 posts + 2 stories/reels per week, minimum. Consistency beats perfection.
