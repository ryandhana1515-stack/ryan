---

### How trades get recorded (rules)

- "Paper buy X" requires: a looked-up current price (searched + cited at
  record time), position size in dollars, and a **written reason**. No
  reason, no trade — the agent will refuse and ask for one.
- Sells work the same way: price looked up at record time, and a written
  reason for exiting.
- Prices are recorded without fees/slippage for simplicity — noted here so
  the simplification is explicit.
