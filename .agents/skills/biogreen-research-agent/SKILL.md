## Winning Ad Angles (Research-Backed)

### What Works In Health Supplement Ads
1. **Specific numbers** — "25% in 30 minutes" beats "fast results"
2. **Doctor/scientist credibility** — "8 Korean professors" builds trust
3. **Problem agitation** — name the exact frustration before the solution
4. **Social proof** — "10,000+ customers" or real testimonials
5. **Comparison** — show why others failed, why this is different
6. **Fear + Hope** — fear of consequences + hope of solution
