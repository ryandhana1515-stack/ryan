FusionTech AI is an AI software and automation company.

We do not position ourselves merely as a website company,
chatbot company, or marketing agency.

Our objective is to build AI-powered company operating systems
that connect a company's people, knowledge, software, sales,
communications, operations and management.

Our flagship concept is:

THE CEO BRAIN

The CEO Brain is an AI operating layer built around an individual
company.

It connects company knowledge, live business information,
AI agents, workflows and business software so that AI can help
the CEO and employees perform actual work.

4. CEO BRAIN
==================================================

The CEO Brain is the intelligence layer above the company.

It should eventually allow a CEO to ask questions such as:

"What happened in my company today?"

"How many new leads came in?"

"Which leads haven't been followed up?"

"What sales opportunities need attention?"

"What projects are late?"

"What customers are unhappy?"

"What invoices require attention?"

"What is my marketing performance?"

"What does my team need from me?"

"What should I focus on today?"

The CEO Brain should retrieve relevant authorized company
information and return a concise management answer.

It should also be able to initiate approved actions through
workflows.

==================================================
14. SECURITY PRINCIPLES
==================================================

Never request customers to paste production passwords into
ordinary AI conversations.

Never hardcode API keys in source code.

Use:

OAuth where available
Secure credentials
Environment variables
Secret management
Least-privilege access

Every client should have isolated credentials and permissions.

Maintain audit logs for important automated actions.

19. AI BEHAVIOUR RULES
==================================================

Every FusionTech AI agent must:

Use available company context before responding.

Never fabricate customer/company information.

Clearly distinguish known information from missing information.

Ask for missing information when necessary.

Use structured data wherever practical.

Record important actions.

Respect permissions.

Escalate sensitive actions.

Keep responses professional and concise.

Protect confidential information.

Never expose API keys, passwords or secrets.

Never make irreversible actions without authorization.

Prefer executing approved workflows over merely explaining what
someone could do.

20. CEO BRAIN'S PRIMARY MISSION
==================================================

The CEO Brain exists to answer three questions:

1. WHAT IS HAPPENING?

2. WHAT NEEDS ATTENTION?

3. WHAT SHOULD HAPPEN NEXT?

It should gather the relevant authorized information, understand
the company's operating rules and present the CEO with the
information required to make decisions.

agent's permissions, the system can execute the workflow through
