# Website Builder — playbook

Write here what every FusionTech-built website must be. The design rules live in
[[Knowledge/Website design standard]]; this note is for behaviour and preferences. Keep it short.

## Always
- Mobile-first, fast, one clear call to action per page, WhatsApp click-to-chat when the customer
  uses WhatsApp, footer with contact placeholders, basic SEO tags.
- Every fact not given by the customer is a clearly marked [PLACEHOLDER]. No invented testimonials,
  awards, prices or client logos.
- Decide the mode first: SME or Medical. Medical is stricter (see the design standard).
- Propose pages a first version needs (3–7), not everything imaginable.

## Never
- No prices, packages, timelines or guarantees anywhere in a brief or a site.
- No publishing to a live domain without Ryan.
- No generic AI look (see the design standard's "Rejected on sight").

## Two variations for every mock-up (Ryan, 2026-09-26)
Every mock-up request produces **two versions** the customer compares side by side:
- **A — Cinematic scroll film site (premium option).** Built with Higgsfield's website builder
  (animated "scroll-scrub" site): a generated cinematic film of the business (~15 s single take,
  3D/video feel) that plays as the visitor scrolls, with the site's sections revealed inside it.
  This is the more expensive tier; say "premium option", never a price.
- **B — Photo-led site.** Built on Lovable: full-bleed generated photography (Higgsfield / Kling),
  cinematic gradient overlays, rich colour, motion (ken-burns, parallax, reveals). The Prestige
  Motors mock-up of 2026-09-26 is the reference.
Both share the same brief, pages, copy rules and QA. John sends both links and says which is the
premium option; the price of either comes only in Ryan's proposal.

## Lessons learned
- 2026-09-26 (Ryan) — The first Prestige Motors mock-up was rejected: flat black boxes, no photos,
  no life. Never again: every section is photographic, colour comes from the imagery, motion is
  visible. Ship the generated photography with the mock-up so it already looks finished.
- 2026-09-26 (Ryan) — Offer the two variations above on every mock-up; the cinematic scroll film
  site is the premium option. Do not describe A as "3D" unless the film actually shows the space
  or product in 3D motion; describe what the visitor will see.
- 2026-09-26 (Ryan) — When a WEBSITE_CREATOR_BRIEF from the Website Intelligence agent is present,
  it is the authoritative input: build the site around its business summary, target customers,
  conversion objective, primary/secondary CTA, sitemap and homepage conversion flow. Do not make a
  generic informational site. Keep every unknown as a visible placeholder ([CLIENT TESTIMONIALS TO BE
  ADDED], [PROJECT IMAGES TO BE PROVIDED], [FINAL PRICING TO BE CONFIRMED]); never invent facts. The
  finished mock-up goes back to John, never to the customer.
