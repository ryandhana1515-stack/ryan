# Discovery questions — the 13 topics (ask progressively, max 3 per reply)

The live agent reads [[Knowledge/Company Discovery — playbook]]; this note is the full question bank. Never all at once; follow what the owner says; unknown stays unknown.

| # | Topic | What we need to learn | Example questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Company profile | company, brand, industry, locations, markets, products/services, size, departments | "What does the business do, and for whom?" · "How many people, in which teams?" |
| 2 | Customers | B2B/B2C, typical customer, segments, repeat business, where the customer list lives | "Who is your typical customer?" · "Where do you keep your customer list today?" |
| 3 | Sales process | lead sources, who receives leads, response time, follow-ups, quotes, where leads are lost | "When a new enquiry comes in, what happens in the first hour?" |
| 4 | Marketing | channels, agencies, content, lead forms | "Where do most enquiries come from?" |
| 5 | Communication channels | WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, email, phone, SMS, website chat, social DMs | "Do customers mostly reach you on WhatsApp or email?" |
| 6 | Current software | CRM, ERP, accounting, POS, booking, spreadsheets, Google/Microsoft, cloud storage, internal apps | "Which software do you use to track customers and money?" |
| 7 | Operations | how work comes in, who does it, assignment, deadlines, approvals, delays | "Once a job is confirmed, who does what?" |
| 8 | Customer service | channels, responders, common questions, complaints, refunds, escalations | "What do customers ask you again and again?" |
| 9 | Finance process | accounting software, invoicing, quotations, collection, expenses, outstanding follow-up | "How do invoices go out and how do you chase late payments?" |
| 10 | People and roles | departments, roles, approval hierarchy, onboarding, SOPs | "Who approves a discount or a refund?" |
| 11 | Management | what the CEO checks daily, reports, hard-to-get numbers, slow decisions, surprises | "What number do you wish you could see every morning?" |
| 12 | Automation signals | repeated daily tasks, copy-paste, forgotten follow-ups, "customers keep asking" | "Which task would you never want to do by hand again?" |
| 13 | Data sources | for each category: available / not / unknown / needs connection / needs import / not required | "Is that in a system we can connect to, or in a file?" |

## Never ask
Passwords or logins · "send us all your data" · prices or budget in the first conversation unless the owner raises it · health, payroll or ID data without a stated purpose.
