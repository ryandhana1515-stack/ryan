# 05 · Property marketing and advertising rules (Singapore)

Part of [[70_Industry_Packs/Singapore_Property/00_Index]]. Used with [[10_Agents/03_Marketing_Growth]],
[[10_Agents/04_Creative_Studio]] and [[cinematic-website]]; the release gate is [[10_Agents/15_Security_Governance_QA]]. The
current rule text and its source are in [[70_Industry_Packs/Singapore_Property/03_Regulation_Register]] (the CEA, Controller of
Housing and PDPC rows).

## Agent ads (CEA)
- The ad must include:
  - the agent's name;
  - the CEA registration number;
  - the contact number;
  - anything else the agency's rules require.
- CEA's ethical advertising guideline has required genuine photographs of the property (for example the actual
  interior and view). Therefore:
  - AI renovation concepts must **never** replace real listing photos, or imply that the unit looks like that today.
  - Real photos come first.
  - AI images go in a clearly labelled section. The label, **"AI renovation concept — for illustration only, not
    the actual unit condition"**, sits ON the image itself.
- Before any agent publishes AI concept images in a listing or ad, check CEA's **current** guidance on digitally
  altered, virtually staged and AI images. If it is unclear, treat it as AMBER, and recommend that the agent confirm
  with their agency or with CEA.
- No misleading claims (returns, yields, "best", "cheapest", titles like "King of…") unless they are substantiated.
- Get the owner's consent before marketing a property.

## Developers (Controller of Housing)
- Artist's impressions and show-flat visuals are labelled as such.
- Follow the Controller of Housing advertising rules.

## Messages and platforms
- SMS and calls: PDPA consent plus a Do Not Call registry check.
- Respect each platform's housing and real-estate ad policies (Meta, TikTok, Google, YouTube).
- Check whether a special ad category applies in the target market.

## Ad asset pack (one per listing or concept)
- [ ] Images in 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9
- [ ] A 15–30 s vertical video. Either a Ken Burns or scroll cut from the renders, or a Higgsfield/Kling shot package
  through the cinematic agent. **Get approval before spending credits.**
- [ ] Headline and primary-text variants
- [ ] The CEA details block (agent name, CEA registration number, contact number)
- [ ] The AI label on every AI image
- [ ] UTM links into the lead form → CRM ([[10_Agents/07_CRM_Architect|ATLAS]])

## Publish checklist (checkpoint 4, agent §21)
- [ ] Real photos come first; there are no AI images in the photo gallery.
- [ ] Every AI image carries the on-image label.
- [ ] CEA's current AI / virtual-staging guidance has been re-checked (register row date ≤ 90 days old).
- [ ] The CEA details block is present.
- [ ] There are no unsubstantiated claims, and no prices, PSF, yields or "sold out" unless they come from the
  agent/developer with a source.
- [ ] The owner's consent is recorded.
- [ ] The agent confirms. **Nothing goes live without that.**
