## Rules for using this register (agent §7)
- **Never invent Singapore regulations.** This table is a starting point.
- **VERIFIED** means that on the "last verified" date, the rule was read on the official page linked in the row.
- **NEEDS VERIFICATION** means one of two things: no official page confirmed the rule, or only part of it was
  confirmed. It must not be used with a customer until it is verified.
- If "last verified" is more than 90 days old, re-verify the row before using it (KNOWLEDGE mode: "Refresh the
  Regulation Register").
- Money, legal and eligibility questions get factual information only, never advice. Point the person to the
  authority, a lawyer, a bank or a CEA-registered agent.
- Refresh 1 did not read the Acts' text on Singapore Statutes Online (sso.agc.gov.sg). The rows cite the authority's
  own pages.
- Figures are SGD. Stamp duty and financing figures are shown exactly as the authority states them.
