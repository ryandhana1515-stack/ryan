## Rules for using this register (agent §7)
- **Never invent Singapore regulations.** This table is a starting point.
- **VERIFIED** means that on the "last verified" date, the rule was read on the official page linked in the row.
- **NEEDS VERIFICATION** means one of two things: no official page confirmed the rule, or only part of it was
  confirmed. It must not be used with a customer until it is verified.
- If "last verified" is more than 90 days old, re-verify the row before using it (KNOWLEDGE mode: "Refresh the
  Regulation Register").
- Money, legal and eligibility questions get factual information only, never advice. Point the person to the
  authority, a lawyer, a bank or a CEA-registered agent.
- Refresh 1 did not read the Acts' text on Singapore Statutes Online (sso.agc.gov.sg). The rows cite the authority's
  own pages.
- Figures are SGD. Stamp duty and financing figures are shown exactly as the authority states them.

## Where the official pages differ from the agent file's baseline
The agent file (§5, §7, §8) is Ryan's text, verbatim, and is not edited. Where the official source says something
different, **the register wins**:

| Agent file says | The official page says | Row |
|---|---|---|
| New flats: bathroom/**kitchen** floor finishes can't be replaced | **Toilet** floor and wall finishes only, for 3 years from the block's completion date. Laying new finishes over the old ones is allowed. | R16 |
| HS: drilling not allowed | Drilling the internal faces to ≤ 50 mm is **permitted** for removable fixtures, and so is painting. Hacking, and altering the door, are not. | R17 |
| Registered (DRC) contractors are needed for permit works | DRC contractors are needed for **all** HDB renovations, including works that need no permit. | R18 |
| MSR applies to HDB flats and ECs | MSR applies to HDB flats, and to ECs **whose MOP has not expired**. | R05 |
| GCBs are in "gazetted" GCB Areas | URA calls them "designated" GCBAs (there are 39). Only bungalow developments are allowed in them. | R24 |
| NEA sets renovation noise and hours for private homes | NEA regulates **landed** A&A works only, with noise limits rather than hours. For condos, the MCST decides; for HDB flats, HDB does. | R36 |
| Licensed plumber for all plumbing | For regulated works, yes. The listed simple works may be done by a handyman. | R35 |
| Approved window contractor | An approved window contractor (RW01) **or** a GB1/GB2 builder, either way using a certified installer. | R28 |
