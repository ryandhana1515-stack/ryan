# 02 · Authority map: who governs what

Part of [[70_Industry_Packs/Singapore_Property/00_Index]]. Each rule an authority sets is listed, with its source and
date, in [[70_Industry_Packs/Singapore_Property/03_Regulation_Register]].

| Authority | Governs | Official site |
|---|---|---|
| **HDB** | HDB flats: renovation permits and guidelines, registered renovation contractors (Directory of Renovation Contractors), MOP, eligibility, resale, rental | hdb.gov.sg |
| **URA** | Planning permission, Master Plan / zoning, landed housing guidelines, GFA rules, conservation, the minimum stay for private-property rentals | ura.gov.sg |
| **BCA** | Building and structural works, plans approval, Qualified Persons, window safety and approved window contractors, accessibility | bca.gov.sg |
| **SCDF** | Fire safety; household and storey shelters (the HS walls and door cannot be altered) | scdf.gov.sg |
| **SLA** | Land titles, strata titles, state land, cadastral and survey information | sla.gov.sg |
| **CEA** | Property agents, practice and advertising guidelines, CEA registration | cea.gov.sg |
| **EMA** | Electrical installation work by Licensed Electrical Workers (LEW); gas | ema.gov.sg |
| **PUB** | Sanitary and plumbing works by licensed plumbers; water | pub.gov.sg |
| **NEA** | Renovation noise and working hours (private property); environmental rules | nea.gov.sg |
| **MCST / BMSMA** | Condo by-laws, renovation deposits, exclusive-use areas (Building Maintenance and Strata Management Act) | sso.agc.gov.sg |
| **IRAS** | Stamp duties (BSD, ABSD, SSD); property tax | iras.gov.sg |
| **MAS** | Financing rules (TDSR, MSR, LTV) | mas.gov.sg |
| **CPF** | CPF use for housing | cpf.gov.sg |
| **Controller of Housing** (under URA) | Licensed developers; the sale and advertising of uncompleted units (Housing Developers Act) | ura.gov.sg |
| **PDPC** | Personal data (PDPA) and the Do Not Call rules for marketing | pdpc.gov.sg |

## Routing rule for the agent
- A question that turns on a rule goes to the register first.
- If the register row is stale (last verified more than 90 days ago) or marked **NEEDS VERIFICATION**, re-check the
  authority's official page before answering.
- If it still can't be verified, name the authority and its page, and stop short of a definitive answer (agent §7).
