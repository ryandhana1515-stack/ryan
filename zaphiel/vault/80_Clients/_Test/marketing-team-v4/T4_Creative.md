## 3. Script: Angle A, hook 1 (25 s, 9:16, sound-off friendly)
Destination: **WhatsApp click-to-chat** (fits John). 18+ targeting (Meta rule for micropigmentation, T3 §6).

| Time | Line (voiceover) | Caption on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **HOOK:** "Everyone shows you brows five minutes after. This is day thirty." | DAY 30. Not day 0. |

## 6. Ready prompts
**a) Phone shot list (for the studio; free)**
1. Healed brow, day 30: daylight by a window, phone at eye level 25 cm away, 1x lens, no beauty filter, 5 s slow push-in.
2. Day 0, day 7 and day 30 photos: the same spot, light, angle and distance each time. Put a sticky note with the day number in the corner.
3. The artist writes the price card by hand: top-down, 8 s.
4. WhatsApp screen recording on a staff phone: open the chat, send a sample selfie (staff member, with consent), 5 s.

**b) Canva price-card carousel (Angle B), 1080×1350, 3 slides**
"Slide 1: headline 'One price. No packages.' Slide 2: [ASK RYAN: price incl. GST] + bullet list of what's included
[ASK RYAN]. Slide 3: 'Send a selfie on WhatsApp → see your shape first'. Clean layout, brand colours [ASK RYAN], large
type, no stock faces, no before/after."

**c) Higgsfield b-roll (optional; only after the cost card and Ryan's yes)**
"Calm, bright brow studio interior, morning light through sheer curtains, neat tray of sterile tools and pigment cups on a
white counter, slow dolly-in, 5 s, 9:16, photoreal, soft natural colours."
Negative: "people, faces, eyebrows, before/after, text, logos, watermarks, medical setting, blood."

**d) ElevenLabs voice (fallback only; needs a paid plan for ads)**
Voice Design: "Warm, calm Singaporean English female voice, early 30s, friendly and honest, not salesy." Stability 0.5,
similarity 0.75, style 0.2, speed 1.0. Script = the voice lines in §3. Never clone anyone's voice except the owner's own,
with consent.

**e) ffmpeg crops + captions (free; run in code)**
`ffmpeg -i master_9x16.mp4 -vf "crop=iw:iw*5/4,scale=1080:1350,subtitles=captions.srt" feed_4x5.mp4`, plus the same
command with `crop=iw:iw,scale=1080:1080` for 1:1 (the 9:16 master is 1080×1920, so both crops cut height, not width).
