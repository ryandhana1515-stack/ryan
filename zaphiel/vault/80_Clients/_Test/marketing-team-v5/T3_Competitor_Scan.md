## 2. Patterns across the market (RESEARCH, built from the 2026-10-12 ad capture and the 2026-10-14 refresh)

1. **Trial-price ladder.** Entry trials run from $99 to $298, against "usual" prices of $398–$860. Premium studios (Browhaus, Sleekbrow) sit at $470–$1,900. Prices tiered by artist (specialist/master, senior/master) are common.
2. **The touch-up is the hidden variable.** Some include one touch-up (within 2–4 months), some charge for it (Sleekbrow, per Miss Lobang), and some trials exclude it. Customers name this exact fear: "touch up need to pay more" (Reddit search excerpt, 2026-10-12).
3. **One outcome dominates:** "stop drawing your brows / wake up with brows done". Nearly every advertiser says it, so on its own it no longer differentiates.
4. **"Natural, not fake" reassurance is near-universal**, often aimed at the cartoon-brow fear in Singlish and Mandarin ads.
5. **"No hard sell" is now table stakes** (True Vine, Sleekbrow, Jac Glow, BP, and Brow Revolution's own ad). It is a promise without proof.
6. **Proof is mostly volume** (review counts, "1,000 / 15,000 clients"), awards, and fresh "immediately after" photos. **Healed-over-time proof is rare** in what was readable. Videos were not viewable, so this is partly not verified.
7. **WhatsApp click-to-chat is the dominant CTA.** Next come Messenger, Instagram profile, dedicated offer pages (Citi, BP, Arch Angel) and one e-commerce checkout (Browhaus).
8. **Risky claims are common:** "Guaranteed", "painless / pain-free", "1st and only", "Singapore's leading / best", and prices quoted before GST.
9. **Age gates appear in T&Cs** (Browhaus 18+, La Vida 21+). Meta requires 18+ targeting for micropigmentation ads (see compliance).
10. **Channels split.** Mass-market studios run paid social. Premium studios such as Sleekbrow lean on Google Maps, reviews and editorial "best of" lists (Daily Vanity, Miss Lobang).

## 4. Compliance notes (official sources; not legal advice)

1. **ASAS superlatives and substantiation.** Under SCAP clause 1.1 (3rd ed., Feb 2008, read on 2026-10-12: https://asas.org.sg/Portals/0/SCAP%202008_1.pdf), claims of objective fact "should be capable of substantiation", with proof ready "for immediate production". So **"Singapore's leading", "best", "No.1", "1st and only" need hard proof or must go.** That includes Brow Revolution's own live line "Singapore's leading eyebrow embroidery". ASAS ran a public consultation on a revised code that closed on 19 Apr 2024 (search summary, 2026-10-14); **whether a new edition is in force is not verified.** Check https://asas.org.sg/About/Code
2. **Meta personal-attributes policy.** Meta does not allow ads that "assert or imply" personal attributes of the audience, including via "you/your" ([Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/personal-attributes), seen via search on 2026-10-14). Meta's ad standards also bar copy that implies negative self-perception to promote cosmetic procedures (cited in the v4 brief, 2026-10-12). **Avoid lines like "Are you insecure about your thin brows?"** Talk about the service and the process instead (for example, "Brow mapping for sparse brows: how it works").
3. **Meta health and wellness policy.** Micropigmentation is listed as a cosmetic procedure, so **ads must target 18+**. Before/after transformation content falls under the same restriction ([Meta Health & Wellness](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/), 2026-10-12).
4. **No fake before/after.** Use only real Brow Revolution clients, with written consent, the same lighting and angle, no filters or retouching of the brow area, and dated where possible. Never use competitor or stock before/after images. The CCS beauty guidance says result claims must not be "unsubstantiated" ([CCS](https://www.ccs.gov.sg/consumer-protection/legislation-and-guidelines/fair-trading-practices-for-beauty-industry/), 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-14).
5. **Real reviews only.** Review counts and star ratings must match a public, checkable source on the day the ad runs. Under SCAP clause 3.2, obsolete testimonials "should not be used". Never reuse another business's testimonials, and never write composite or invented reviews.
6. **Prices and discounts.** If the business is GST-registered, prices must be shown GST-inclusive ([IRAS](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-(gst)/basics-of-gst/invoicing-price-display-and-record-keeping/displaying-and-quoting-prices), 2026-10-12). A "U.P." or "usual price" must be genuine, and a discount "cannot be offered indefinitely" (SCAP 8.1). Anything called "free" must disclose any cost (SCAP 7.1–7.3).
7. **No pressure and no risky prepayment.** CCS guidance says businesses must not "pressure their customers into buying". MTI's 5 Aug 2026 reply cites CCS action against 15 beauty and wellness firms over prepaid-package practices ([MTI](https://www.mti.gov.sg/newsroom/written-reply-to-pq-on-enforcement-against-beauty-and-wellness-businesses-under-consumer-protection--fair-trading--act-for-pressure-selling-or-unfair-practices/), search summary 2026-10-14).
