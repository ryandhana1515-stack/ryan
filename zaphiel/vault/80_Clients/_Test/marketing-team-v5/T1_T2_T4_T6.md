## T6 — "Where it went wrong" (DEMO account: the 5 scenarios as one brow studio, 7 days)
**Summary:** The account spent S$1,850 for 168 leads (CPL S$11.01, PLATFORM-REPORTED), but only 49 were qualified (29%,
CRM-CONFIRMED in this DEMO) and 7 became sales (S$2,716 revenue, ROAS 1.47). The money leaks are a tired reel, a broken
WhatsApp/tracking link and an instant form that brings cheap but unqualified leads. The founder-story ad works. Fix
tracking first: until then no budget changes.

**Funnel scorecard (account):** 1 Delivery 🟢 (CPM 10.11) · 2 Attention 🟢 (hook rate 32%) · 3 Click 🟢 (link CTR 1.42%)
· 4 Destination 🟢 (6.4% of clicks become leads) · 5 Lead 🟢 (CPL 11.01 vs target 15) · 6 Quality 🔴 (29% qualified vs
40%) · 7 Sale 🟡 (close rate 14%; median first reply 95 min vs 15 target).

**Top 3 money leaks (engine):**
1. Brow reel "Wake up with perfect brows": link CTR fell 58% while frequency rose 125%; CPL 10.71 → 30. New hooks on the
   same angle (S$450 spent).
2. WhatsApp offer "Free brow consult": leads fell to 0 for 2 days (4/day before) while clicks held. Fix the WhatsApp
   link / tracking (S$350).
3. Instant form "Win free brows": CPL 6.25 but only 13% qualified (target 40%). Qualifying questions / higher-intent
   form (S$350).

**What's working:** Founder story "Why I only do natural brows": SCALE (CPL at target 3 days running). Under v5 the raise
waits: performance changes are **suspended** while tracking is broken (engine: `suspended: true`, 0 proposals).

**14-day fix plan:** Day 1 fix the WhatsApp link and test events (TITAN/ATLAS) · Day 1 John replies within 15 min
(ATLAS follow-up rule) · Day 2 new hooks for the reel (Creative Brain; approval for any paid generation) · Day 2 add 2
qualifying questions to the form (approval) · Day 4, after 48 h of clean tracking: proposal "founder story +20%"
(approval) · Day 7 and Day 14 reviews.

**How to confirm the fix:** WhatsApp ad back to ≥3 leads/day with clicks steady; reel link CTR back above 1%; form
qualified rate ≥40%; median first reply ≤15 min.

**Data gaps:** DEMO data; no real account connected; attribution window and conversion event to confirm per account.
