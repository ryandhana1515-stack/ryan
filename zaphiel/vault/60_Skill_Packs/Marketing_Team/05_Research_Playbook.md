# Market Research Playbook

Everything here is labelled **RESEARCH**. It is kept separate from our own performance data and always carries its
source and date.

## 1. Competitor ad scan
1. Find 5–10 real competitors: Google search, Google Maps, review sites, and the client's own list.
2. Search the public ad libraries:
   - **Meta Ad Library** (Facebook and Instagram; all active ads are public);
   - **TikTok Creative Center** (Top Ads and trends);
   - **Google Ads Transparency Center** (Google and YouTube ads).
   These pages are often JavaScript-only. When a page can't be read automatically, record the search URL for a person to
   open and say so. **Never log in to scrape.**
3. For each ad, record:
   - start date and how long it has been running;
   - format;
   - angle;
   - hook (first line or first 3 seconds);
   - offer;
   - CTA;
   - landing type (form, WhatsApp, page).
4. **A long-running ad is a SIGNAL** that it may be working. It is not proof.

## 2. Voice of customer
Read public reviews (Google, marketplaces), comments and forums (Reddit, HardwareZone). Collect:
- pain points;
- fears and objections;
- the exact words customers use (short quotes with links).

These words become hooks and scripts.

## 3. Trends
Use the platforms' creative centres for trending formats and sounds. Check the commercial-use licensing of any sound
before an ad uses it.

## 4. Output
- **Research brief** (plain English): competitors, patterns (angles, hooks, offers, formats), voice of customer, compliance
  notes, gaps and opportunities, and **3 angles to test**.
- **Swipe file:** a table of competitor | link | format | angle | hook | offer | CTA | landing | notes | verified?
- **What I could not access.**

## Rules
- Take inspiration only. Never copy competitors' creative, claims or branding.
- Never invent competitors, prices, reviews or ads.
- Beauty and health: note the ASAS code and the platform policies on claims and before/after.
