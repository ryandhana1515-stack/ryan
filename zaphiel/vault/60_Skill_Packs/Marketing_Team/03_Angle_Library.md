# Angle Library

## Angles (each angle is a reason to care)

| Angle | Use when | Truth rule |
|---|---|---|
| Pain / problem | The customer feels a clear daily pain | Describe it in the customer's own words (from research) |
| Desired outcome | The result is easy to picture | Only outcomes the service can deliver |
| Social proof | Real reviews or clients exist | **Real and consented only.** No invented testimonials |
| Authority / expertise | Certifications, years, training are real | Show the proof (certificate, portfolio) |
| Demonstration / how it works | The process removes fear | Real footage of the real process is best |
| Objection-busting | One objection blocks the sale (pain, price, "looks fake") | Answer honestly; no medical claims |
| Offer / urgency | There is a genuine offer or real deadline | Truthful only; real end dates |
| Comparison vs alternatives | Customers compare options (DIY, competitors, doing nothing) | No naming or attacking competitors; facts only |
| Founder / behind the scenes | The owner's story builds trust | Real story |
| Local relevance | "For busy Singapore professionals", nearby MRT, local habits | Accurate local facts |
| Transformation | Before/after is real, consented and compliant | **Never fake before/after**; follow the platform's and ASAS rules for beauty/health |

## Hook formulas (the first 1–3 seconds or the first line)
- **Question:** "Still drawing your brows every morning?"
- **Bold statement:** "Your brows shouldn't take 15 minutes a day."
- **Callout:** "If you're a busy mum in Singapore…"
- **Mistake:** "Stop doing this before your brow appointment."
- **Result-first** (only if true): show the real finished result, then rewind.
- **POV / UGC:** "POV: you wake up and your brows are already done."
- **Pattern interrupt (visual):** an unexpected first frame, movement or text that breaks the scroll.

## Script structure (20–30 seconds)
1. **HOOK** (0–3 s): one of the formulas above, with on-screen text.
2. **PROBLEM / DESIRE** (3–8 s): the pain or wish, in their words.
3. **PROOF / DEMO** (8–18 s): process, real results, credentials.
4. **OFFER** (18–24 s): what they get, truthfully.
5. **CTA** (24–30 s): one clear next step (for example "WhatsApp us to book a free consult").

**Captions on screen always.** Assume sound is off in the feed.

## Testing order
ANGLE → HOOK → FORMAT → OFFER → AUDIENCE ([[02_Diagnosis_Playbook]] section 4). Three angles × three hooks = nine
variants is the usual first test.
