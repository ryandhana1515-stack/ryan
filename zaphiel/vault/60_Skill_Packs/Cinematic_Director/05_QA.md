# QA: check before spending, check before handing over

## Before any video credits are spent (on the stills)
- [ ] The look bible is the same in every still (set, light direction, lens, colour).
- [ ] **Products:** every part exists in the real product (customer photo or spec). No invented parts, logos or
      text. The exploded still is an edit of the hero still, and the parts are identical.
- [ ] **Medical:** the picture matches the facts table in [[02_Medical_Anatomy_Films]] (chambers, where the
      arteries run, red cells as discs, plaque inside the wall…). No gore, needles, text or extra organs.
- [ ] Phone version (9:16) keeps the subject inside the safe zone, with room for the copy.

## On every film
- [ ] The first frame equals the first still and the last frame equals the tail still (pause on both ends).
- [ ] Parts stay rigid. No melting, no new parts appearing, no flicker.
- [ ] Movement is slow and readable when scrubbed backward (that is the reassembly on the site).
- [ ] No black frames, no cut, no fade at either end.
- [ ] No watermark left on the final clip (or `crop: "kling"` is set in media.json).
- [ ] The scene's last frame is the next scene's first frame.

## Before hand-off
- [ ] Permanent URLs kept (Kling links expire after 24 hours).
- [ ] Medical: "Illustration for education only" label noted; captions `[VERIFY WITH CLINIC]`; doctor approval listed
      as the gate before going live.
- [ ] Credits used are recorded against the approved budget.
- [ ] [[06_Prompt_Log]] updated.

Rate each problem BLOCKER / HIGH / MEDIUM / POLISH (doctrine §24). Nothing with a BLOCKER is handed over.
