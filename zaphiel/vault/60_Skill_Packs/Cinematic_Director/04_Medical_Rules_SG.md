# Singapore rules for doctors' and clinics' websites and ads (summary)

Working checklist, not legal advice; the clinic checks the current law text before going live.

- **Authorisation:** the clinic (HCSA licensee) authorises the work; it is responsible for the ads.
- **Banned even if true:** praise and superlatives ("best", "leading", "No. 1", "painless", "guaranteed"),
  comparisons with other clinics, promised results, before/after or "after only" images (generated ones too).
- **Testimonials:** none on clinic sites (the SMC bars doctors from testimonials on media they control).
- **No urgency tactics** (countdowns, "limited slots"), no emotional pressure.
- **AI:** no MOH/SMC rule on AI images or presenters was found; never show an AI person as a doctor or a patient.
  Generated anatomy is labelled "Illustration for education only".
- **Accurate and checkable:** facts the clinic supplies, SMC-registered qualifications as given, evidence kept for
  every claim.
- **Forms (PDPA):** purpose stated before collecting, specific marketing consent, Do Not Call rules (WhatsApp too).

Details and sources: [[60_Skill_Packs/TITAN/12_SG_Rules_Research]] §A (clinics), §E (PDPA), §F (general).
