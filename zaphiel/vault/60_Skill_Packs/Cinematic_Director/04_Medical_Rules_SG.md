# Singapore rules for doctors' and clinics' websites and ads

**Status: being researched with sources** (MOH healthcare-services advertising rules under the Healthcare Services
Act, the SMC Ethical Code and Ethical Guidelines, PDPA for enquiry forms). Until this note is filled, treat every
point below as **UNVERIFIED** and send every claim to the clinic for review.

Working rules until then (from [[02_Medical_Anatomy_Films]] and doctrine §11):
- No patient testimonials, no before/after results, no success rates, no "best", "painless" or "guaranteed".
- Only facts the clinic supplies and approves; doctors' credentials exactly as the clinic gives them.
- Generated anatomy is labelled "Illustration for education only".
- Enquiry forms ask only what is needed, with a PDPA consent line; no medical details in a web form unless the clinic
  has a secure process.
- The clinic's doctor signs off every page, film and caption before anything goes live.
