# Medical & supplement experiences: built for trust

## Sources and accuracy
- **Anatomy:** films made by TITAN 3D Studio follow the facts table in
  [[60_Skill_Packs/Cinematic_Director/02_Medical_Anatomy_Films]]. Any 3D model or illustration from outside is
  **licensed**, with the licence and attribution recorded in the project's `ASSETS.md`.
- **Labels:** "Illustration for education only" on every generated anatomy visual, or "Medically reviewed
  representation" only when the clinic's qualified clinician has reviewed it in writing.
- **Clinician review:** the clinic's doctor approves the anatomy, labels, captions, presenter script and every page
  before launch. Record who approved and when in `web/STATUS.md`.

## Experience patterns (doctors)
The body journey with scroll (body → organ → artery → back out) · hotspots/labels as HTML · procedure steps
(illustrative, only procedures the clinic offers) · plaque/blood-flow as clearly illustrative animation · the presenter
video section · doctor profiles with real, verified credentials · consultation booking/WhatsApp. Non-gory. Reduced
motion = static diagrams.

## Presenter rules
- Best: the clinic's real doctor, filmed (phone is fine), speaking a script the clinic approved.
- An AI presenter is allowed only as an **illustrative presenter**, labelled "AI presenter" on screen and near the
  player. It never claims to be the doctor or a patient.
- Voice cloning only of the doctor's own voice, with recorded consent.

## Never
- Invent clinical outcomes, efficacy, success rates, diagnostic claims, before/after evidence or supplement
  benefits.
- Show patient testimonials or patient information.
- Present synthetic demonstrations as real clinical footage.
- Use "best", "painless", "guaranteed", "No. 1" or comparisons with other doctors.
- Show injection points, dosages or procedure steps the clinic has not supplied.

## Supplements / health products
- Only claims the client can prove and that the rules allow. No disease claims ("cures", "treats", "prevents
  <disease>") for supplements.
- Ingredient facts from the label/spec sheet, never invented.
- Health-product advertising rules (HSA) apply; flag every claim for the client's regulatory review.

## Regulatory review before publishing (flag for the client's expert)
- Singapore healthcare-services advertising rules (MOH, Healthcare Services Act).
- The Singapore Medical Council's ethical code on doctors' information.
- Health-product/supplement advertising rules (HSA).
- Platform ad policies (Meta, TikTok, Google health policies).
- PDPA for every form ([[09_Security_Compliance_Checklists]]).
