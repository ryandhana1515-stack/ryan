# Finance, crypto and banking-style sites

## Two different things
| Buildable by TITAN | Needs licences, security audits and legal review BEFORE any build |
|---|---|
| Information, education and market-data sites; calculators; company sites for licensed firms (with their compliance team) | Wallets, trading, custody, payments, lending, investment advice, anything holding customer money or keys |

Never deploy custody or trading from a website prompt.

## Market data
Reliable sources with permission to display, attribution, and an "updated at" time. Delayed data is labelled as delayed.

## Singapore cautions (verify the current rules on every project)
- **Crypto:** MAS restricts how digital payment token (crypto) services may be marketed to the public in Singapore.
  Every crypto marketing site is flagged for legal review before publishing.
- **Financial promotions:** follow MAS rules and the client's licence conditions; no guaranteed returns; risk
  warnings where required.

## Banking-style UI
Strong authentication (MFA), secure sessions, audit logs, rate limiting, accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA), and nothing
sensitive in the browser.
