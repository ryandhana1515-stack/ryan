# Marketing targets (per business)

Ryan fills these in. The [[10_Agents/Marketing/_index|Marketing Brain]] and, later, the Atlas Marketing module judge
campaigns against them. Where a box still says **ASK RYAN**, the agents compare each brand with its own previous period
instead and say so. They never invent a target.

How to fill:
- Write a number with its currency (for example `SGD 15`) or a percentage.
- Leave a line out if it does not apply (for example ROAS for a business that does not sell online).
- Newest date wins: add a dated line rather than deleting the old one.
