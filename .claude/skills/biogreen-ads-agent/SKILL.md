## KPIs To Track
- **CTR:** Target > 2% (Facebook), > 1.5% (TikTok)
- **CPC:** Target < $0.80
- **CPM:** Target < $15
- **ROAS:** Target > 2x break-even, > 3x profitable
- **CPA (Cost Per Purchase):** Target < 30% of product price
