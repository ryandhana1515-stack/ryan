Chapter 10

Why Nitric Oxide Matters

Nitric oxide (NO) is a signalling molecule involved in many normal physiological processes — including blood-vessel signalling, circulation and cellular communication. It is part of how the body maintains everyday vitality.

Circulation

Involved in normal vasodilation and healthy blood flow.

Cellular Communication

Acts as a messenger molecule between cells.

Everyday Vitality

Supports normal energy and physical performance.

Normal Immune Function

Participates in the body’s normal immune processes.

Cognitive Function

Involved in normal signalling within the nervous system.

Chapter 11

Nitric Oxide and Age

Research suggests natural nitric-oxide production may decline with age. Supporting healthy NO levels is one part of maintaining overall wellness through the decades.

20s

Natural production is typically at its peak.

30s

Production may begin to gradually decline.

40s

Lifestyle support becomes increasingly relevant.

50s

Many adults look to diet and exercise for support.

60+

Maintaining healthy habits matters most.
