Our Technology

PicoSure Laser – The Gold Standard

PicoSure is the world's most advanced aesthetic laser, delivering ultrashort bursts of energy in trillionths of a second. It shatters pigment particles into microscopic fragments that your body naturally eliminates.

  • Photoacoustic effect — no heat damage to surrounding skin
  • FDA-cleared technology trusted by clinics worldwide
  • Faster results with fewer sessions than traditional lasers
  • Effective on all pigment colours including red, blue and grey
  • No pigmentation or de-pigmentation side effects
