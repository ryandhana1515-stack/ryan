About the Technology

What is PicoSure Laser?

PicoSure is a next-generation picosecond laser that delivers ultrashort pulses of energy measured in trillionths of a second (picoseconds). This revolutionary speed allows it to shatter pigment particles far more effectively — and safely — than traditional Q-switched nanosecond lasers.

Instead of relying on heat (photothermal effect), PicoSure uses a photoacoustic effect — a pressure wave that fragments pigment into microscopic dust-like particles. Your body's immune system then naturally eliminates these tiny particles over time.

  • Ultrashort pulses in picoseconds (trillionths of a second)
  • Photoacoustic effect — minimal heat, maximum pigment shattering
  • Less thermal damage to surrounding healthy skin
  • Faster and more complete pigment clearance
  • Used by professional aesthetic clinics globally

PicoSure vs Traditional Lasers

Understand why picosecond technology represents a fundamental leap forward in laser aesthetics.

⚡

Speed: Pico vs Nano

Traditional nanosecond lasers fire in billionths of a second. PicoSure fires in trillionths — 100x faster. This speed is what makes all the difference in how cleanly pigment is shattered.

🔥

Less Heat Damage

Because pulses are so short, there is far less heat delivered to surrounding tissue. This means reduced risk of scarring, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, and skin damage.

🌟

Fewer Sessions

The more complete the pigment shattering per session, the fewer total sessions needed. Clients often see significant results after just 3 sessions with PicoSure technology.

🌅

Broader Colour Range

Multiple wavelengths (532nm, 755nm, 1064nm) allow effective treatment of a wide spectrum of pigment colours — including stubborn red, blue, green, and grey tones.

👀

No Extra Consumables

Advanced design means no pigmentation or de-pigmentation side effects and no need for extra consumables after each treatment session.

🎯

Precision Targeting

Adjustable focal spot diameter and interval allow practitioners to precisely target treatment areas — essential for delicate eyebrow embroidery removal.
