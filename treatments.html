Conditions We Treat

What We Can Remove

Our PicoSure laser is effective on a wide range of eyebrow embroidery issues.

🦍

Old Eyebrow Embroidery

Embroidery done years ago that has faded, blurred, or shifted colour. Removal allows you to start fresh with modern, natural-looking techniques.

🌈

Discoloured Pigment

Pigment that has shifted to unwanted tones — red, orange, blue, grey, or green. Our multi-wavelength laser targets and breaks down all colour variations.

📈

Uneven or Asymmetric Brows

Poorly applied embroidery that looks uneven, asymmetric, or simply wrong for your face shape. We help restore balance and symmetry.

🕓

Outdated Styles

Block brows, over-arched shapes, or any style that no longer reflects current trends or your preferences. Removal opens the door to a modern look.

☛

Blurred or Spread Pigment

Over time, pigment can spread beyond its original border. Precise laser targeting removes the spread pigment without affecting surrounding skin.

👷

Partial Correction

Want to lighten rather than fully remove? We can perform partial treatments to fade the existing embroidery enough for correction or re-embroidery.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often before treatment.

Is it painful?

Most clients describe the sensation as a mild rubber-band snap. We apply numbing cream before every session to minimise discomfort.

How many sessions do I need?

Typically 3–8 sessions depending on pigment depth, colour, and age of the embroidery. We will give you a personalised estimate during your consultation.

How long between sessions?

We recommend 6–8 weeks between sessions to allow your body's immune system to fully eliminate the fragmented pigment particles.

Is it safe for all skin types?

Yes. Our PicoSure protocol is safe for all Fitzpatrick skin types. We adjust laser settings based on your individual skin assessment.

Will there be scarring?

With proper technique, scarring is extremely rare. PicoSure's photoacoustic mechanism minimises heat damage, significantly reducing the risk compared to older lasers.

Can I re-embroider after removal?

Yes! Once the area is sufficiently cleared and the skin has fully healed (typically 3–6 months after final session), you are free to have new embroidery done.
